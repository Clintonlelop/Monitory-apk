Accessibility Setup & Activation Instructions

Android requires the device owner to manually authorize any Accessibility Service to maintain complete platform control. To enable the service:

  1. Ensure the Device Manager app is installed on the target device.
  2. Open Settings -> Accessibility on the device.
  3. Locate Device Integrity Assistant in the listed services.
  4. Tap the service and switch the toggle to ON.
  5. Accept the platform consent dialog confirming transparent device management.

To Disable: Navigating to the same settings page and toggling the switch to OFF terminates all accessibility event reporting instantly.
